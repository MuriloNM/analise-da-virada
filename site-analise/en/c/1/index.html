Brazil's runoff reversal, chapter 1 of 9

Where did Lula grow most: where he was already strong or where he was weak?

Where he was weaker. Comparing municipalities in the same state, the larger Lula's first-round share, the less it rose: for every 10 points more, 0.51 points less. The same holds for Flávio: each candidate grew more where he was weaker.
