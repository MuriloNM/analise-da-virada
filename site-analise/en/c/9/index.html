Brazil's runoff reversal, chapter 9 of 9

What else do the data show?

Exploratory, not recorded before the calculations. There were ten questions, in two rounds, asked after seeing the results. The clearest answers: Lula overtook Flávio in 211 municipalities, and Flávio overtook Lula in 1; the other candidates' votes did not tilt the same way: in the polling-station calculation, the net of Augusto Cury's votes tilted to Lula, and the net of Ronaldo Caiado's to Flávio; and, in the first round, just over half of voters aged 70 or over turned out (50.9%).
