Brazil's runoff reversal, chapter 7 of 9

How close did the live projection come to the final result?

Close, and increasingly so: with 10% to 20% of voting machines counted, the live projection gave Lula, on average, 1.06 points less than he got in the end, and Flávio the same amount more; the partial count, without projection, was off by 1.84. From 60% counted onward, the error stayed below 0.1 percentage point.
