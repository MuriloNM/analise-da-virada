Brazil's runoff reversal, chapter 4 of 9

Did turnout change?

It rose: 2.96 million more voters turned out, and abstention fell from 21.08% to 19.22% (−1.86 percentage points). But abstention rose in 9 states: all 7 in the North, Maranhão, and Piauí. Where abstention fell the most, Lula's share did not clearly rise more.
