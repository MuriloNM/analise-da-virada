Brazil's runoff reversal, chapter 2 of 9

Where did Flávio grow?

In votes, in most of the country: Flávio had 3.72 million more votes in the runoff, in 21 of the 27 states and in 4,624 of the 5,571 municipalities. As Lula had more (10.38 million), Flávio's share of the two-candidate vote fell in all 27 states and rose in 160 municipalities.
