What was each region's and state's share of the reversal?

The margin between the two changed by 6.66 million votes in Lula's favor: 42.9% of this change came from the Southeast and 38.4% from the Northeast; São Paulo accounted for 21.0%. The margin moved in Lula's favor in all 27 states.
